## Where it stands

Human chores now count towards milestones. A person keeps checklists of jobs
in the web UI, and the chat agent keeps them over MCP, ticking a job only by
relaying the person's words. A milestone isn't complete while one of its jobs
is unticked, and marking it as shipped with a job open records the checklist
as not shipped. The definition-of-done demo ran in the browser and over MCP,

## What was built

**In the data** (migration `0009`):

- `checklists`, owned by the project or an initiative with the same CHECKs as
  milestones and roadmaps.
- `jobs`, with a title, a note, a dense position, and `ticked_by`, `ticked_at`,
  `ticked_via` and `ticked_quote`. CHECKs keep the tick fields together, and a
  tick through the chat agent must carry a quote.
- `checklist` is allowed in `milestone_members` and as a snapshot leaf, and
  `milestone_snapshots.done_at_lock` records whether each checklist was done
  when its milestone shipped.

**In the store:** `CreateChecklist`, `AddJob`, `EditJob`, `PlaceJob`,
`RemoveJob` and `TickJob`, each audited against the checklist in the caller's
transaction, with row locks so ticks and positions can't race. A milestone's
leaves are now features and checklists: `Progress.Total` and `Done` count
both, while `Leaves` stays the features, so tokens and costs are unchanged.
`LockMilestone` snapshots checklists and writes `not_shipped`, and G4's
sentences count items.

**In the web UI:**

- **New checklist** in the owner's plan section, and the owner's checklists
  listed there with their status and an **Edit** button.
- **The checklist page**, `/ui/c/{id}`: the jobs as plain checkboxes. Press a
  box to tick or untick, or use **Tick with a note…**. Each ticked job says who
  ticked it and when, with the person's words when the chat agent relayed it.
- **The checklist editor**, a modal like the milestone editor: add, change,
  reorder and remove jobs. It opens from the owner's page and from the
  checklist page.
- **Add to a milestone…** on the checklist page, a checklist branch in the
  milestone picker, and a checklist row in the milestone editor showing "3 of
  5 jobs ticked".

**Over MCP**, eight tools, making thirty-three in all:

- `create_checklist`, `add_job`, `rename_job`, `move_job`, `remove_job`,
  `list_checklists` and `get_checklist`, as planning;
- `relay_tick_job`, which ticks or unticks on the person's quoted words,
  audited `via: mcp`.

`add_milestone_member` and `remove_milestone_member` take `checklist`.

## Where the brief and the documents differed

The brief said the documents win, and to record these.

- **"A checklist page where you can add, rename, reorder and remove jobs."**
  DESIGN-010 §6 says checklists are "edited in a modal from its owner's page"
  and jobs "are ticked on the checklist page". Both are met: ticking is on the
  page, and editing is in a modal that the page and the owner's page both open
  (SD-5).
- **"`relay_tick_job` ticks or unticks."** DEC-006 Amendment 1 names only "a
  ticked job", and says a relay not on its list needs a decision. The untick is
  built and put to Sam (SD-10).
- **"Authoring tools: rename, reorder or remove a job."** DESIGN-010 §17a item
  4 says "create". These are built, limited and put to Sam (SD-7).
- **"Progress and the shipping gate count jobs"** (roadmap §11). This is
  counted per checklist, as DESIGN-010 §6 counts deliverables (SD-1).
- **`entity.html` changes limited to includes.** No change was made there at
  all, so the milestone *page* shows a checklist member as "checklist" without
  its jobs count, which the milestone *editor* does show. "New checklist" isn't
  in the owner page's "more" menu either; it is in the plan section beside New
  milestone and New roadmap.

## Decisions taken in the build

- **Job events are audited against the checklist**, with the job's id and
  title in the payload, so a checklist's history is one query and survives a
  job's removal. `checklist.created` is filed against the checklist, as
  `milestone.created` is against its milestone.
- **A job is named by id or by exact title within its checklist.** A title
  two jobs share is refused with the count, so a relay never ticks "the first
  of two", and a job id from another checklist is refused.
- **A tick through `mcp` without a quote is refused in the store and by the
  table**, not only by the tool.
- **`add_job` with a position writes two audit rows**, `job.added` and
  `job.moved`, because it is an add followed by a move.
- **Every job form carries `checklist_id`**, so acting on a job someone else
  has removed brings the page up to date with a sentence rather than an error.

## Found and fixed on the way

**Closing an editor sent the person to the wrong page** (commit `a66faae`).
Every page body is `hx-boost`ed, so after a form post HTMX put the post's
route, such as `/ui/milestone/new`, in the address bar. An editor that closes
after a change reloads the page, and that address redirects to `/ui`. This came
with M4's editors and affected milestones and roadmaps too.

Pages rendered for a boosted post now send `HX-Push-Url` with their own
address. That covers the project, initiative, feature, milestone and checklist
pages. It is in SPEC-014 as FR-4.7, and `TestUIChecklists` checks the header.
The M4 walkthrough didn't hit the bug, because its script navigated with
`page.goto` after closing each editor.

   roadmaps (M4 follow-up 1). This is more pressing now. An empty checklist
   the chat agent created and added to a milestone keeps that milestone short
   of complete, and nothing but taking it out can fix that.

   as the editor does, once `entity.html` is free to change. Moving the row
   into a partial in `plan.html` would do it.
3. **Owner and checklist pages don't refresh live** when the chat agent
   changes them. FR-4.6 makes a stale page safe, not current.
4. **The dashboard doesn't show project-owned checklists**, and the JSON API
   and command line can't add a checklist to a milestone. The command line is
   being retired, so the second may never matter.
5. **A shipped nested milestone shows as done in its parent** even if a
   checklist in it has an unticked job. This predates M5; the parent's own
   count is right.
