## Where it stands

The chat agent now has its own seat.
- It can write a spec or plan with a person, add the file, and hand it in
  with `submit_for_review`, without quoting anyone. The independent reviewer
  decides.
- A spec written this way skips spec writing when the feature is sent. The
  send screen says so, and names who wrote it and who approved it.
- Every document now records who wrote it, and every verdict records who gave
  it and how. The document page shows both in one line under its heading. MCP
  results carry them too, and so does the timeline, where it fits.
- The relay tools take document IDs. The milestone, roadmap and checklist
  results give `MS-`, `RM-` and `CL-` IDs. The plan lists and checklist pages
  show them, and `/ui/id/CL-…` works.
- The chat agent can read a feature's timeline (`get_timeline`) and any agent
  run (`get_agent_run`).

The suite is green. The demo ran with no AI provider. The one step that needs
a model, the agent reviewer's verdict, is proved by
`TestChatWrittenSpecFlowsToStartBuilding` with the mock provider.

## The audit

The brief asked for an audit first. SPEC-017 §2 has it in full. In short, M3
had built the relays, the send screen's "already done" steps, and direct
human approval. What was missing:
- a way for the chat agent to hand in its own work without a quote;
- any record of writers and verdicts that a page could show;
- the ID follow-ups M8 left;
- M6's two read tools;
- a test of the whole §5a path.

## What was built

**Submitting from chat (FR-1).** `submit_for_review` takes a path or an ID
and runs the page's Submit as the chat agent.
- The submission's own audit row says `via: mcp`.
- It refuses, each with a sentence:
  - a document already in review ("a fresh review is a person's call");
  - an approved or superseded one;
  - a type with no template;
  - a draft its author agent is revising;
  - one with a question in the Inbox;
  - a revision of a feature being built;
  - a draft that fails validation, listing what to fix.
- Its answer says what happens next, in the project's own terms: the
  reviewer and model, the hold, agent review switched off, a plan's
  decomposition, or a design waiting for a person.
- `relay_review_request` keeps its quote.

**Writers and verdicts (FR-2).** Migration `0011` adds `document_writers` and
`document_verdicts`.
- **Writers** are recorded where the writing happens: an author agent, with
  its role, model and run; attach and adopt, from chat or the UI; `doc add`;
  starter designs; Revise; a revision opened by an issue; and the cascade.
- **Verdicts** are recorded in the approve and send-back paths: agents with
  their run, people with how the verdict came, held approvals, releases,
  escalation answers with the review they ruled on, and approvals by
  adoption.
- The transition's audit row now carries `verdict_by`, `via` and
  `dispatch_id`. This closes M6 follow-up 2.
- One place builds the sentences (`internal/server/provenance.go`). The
  document page (`provenance.html`, one include), MCP results
  (`written_by`, `last_verdict`) and the send screen all use it.
- The writer line reads across revisions, so a revised spec still says who
  first wrote it.
- **Existing documents** are read back in two passes. Migration `0011`
  records what the trail pins down. `BackfillProvenance`, at server start,
  credits the rest using the configured actor names. Both mark rows
  *inferred*; anything else says "Who wrote this wasn't recorded."

**The timeline.** A relayed verdict is credited to "a person, relayed by the
chat agent". It reads the chat agent's name from `mcpActor()`.

**IDs (FR-3).**
- `store.Checklist` reads its `public_id`, and there is a lookup by it.
- The relays take document IDs. An ID names its newest revision that isn't
  superseded.
- Milestone, roadmap and checklist results give `id` as the ID and `row_id`
  as the row id. Milestone items carry IDs.
- The plan section, roadmap spine, milestone items and checklist pages show
  IDs.
- `/ui/id/CL-…` redirects.

**Read tools (FR-4).**
- `get_timeline` returns the moments, who caused each, and the runs.
- `get_agent_run` returns a run's conclusion, whether its verdict was
  applied, and a compact transcript. Tool results are left out unless asked
  for.

**The configuration** refuses one actor name for both the chat agent and the
web UI.

## Decisions taken in the build

- **The writer is recorded at registration, not at every edit.** Subutai sees
  a changed file, not who changed it (SPEC-017 §6).
- **`internal/rules` changes by one field.** `FileAuthoredDocument` carries its
  run. The approve and send-back paths gained server-side variants,
  `approveDocumentAs` and `returnForChangesAs`, that take the verdict to
  record. The surfaces that know how an act came call those.
- **The database refuses a relayed verdict with no quote**, as it already
  refuses a relayed tick with no quote.
- **The boot backfill only touches documents older than `0011`.** It reads
  `schema_migrations.applied_at`, so running it at every start adds nothing
  new.
- **Tool results are left out of `get_agent_run` by default.** They are what
  most often carries a secret out of a transcript.
- **The written-by line uses the page head's meta row.** No stylesheet change,
  to keep clear of M9.

## Where the brief conflicted with an approved document, or went further

- **"Submitting … and asking for an early review are planning acts, not
  relayed human verdicts."** DEC-006 Amendment 1 decision 8 and DESIGN-010 §5c
  list "a request for a review" as a relay, which needs a quote. The documents
  win, so this is built as Sam's choice (DoD 8.1), with the dated notes
  above, and not assumed. The two acts are also separated:
  - submitting a draft needs no quote;
  - a fresh review of a document in review keeps its quote.
- **The roadmap's M10 says "Allow a human verdict on any document type"**,
  which the brief's scope didn't list. The audit found it holds in the web UI
  (SPEC-015 FR-5.8). The chat route is deferred to M11, as a choice for Sam.
- **"The timeline where it fits."** The timeline still reads the audit
  trail, so its words and the page's differ slightly (SPEC-017 FR-2.7). That
  is accepted and recorded.
- **"Keep your 'written by' line to one include."** It is one include on the
  document page. Three other small changes touch M9's files:
  - `entity.html` also gains IDs in the milestone page's item list (a
    different page);
  - `docPageData` gains a `Provenance` field;
  - `documents.go` gains `submitDocWith` and `reviseDocBy`.

## Live smoke for Sam

With a real provider, on the smoke project (`scripts/smoke-project.sh`), with
agent spec review **on**:

1. In chat, with the MCP server connected, ask the chat agent to write a spec
   for a described feature under an approved design, commit it, adopt it, and
   submit it for review. It should need no quote.
2. Watch the spec reviewer run. The spec's page should say "Written by the
   chat agent" and, once approved, "Approved by the spec reviewer (*model*)",
   with a link to the review.
3. Open **Send to development**. Both spec steps should say they are skipped.
   Send.
4. Watch the plan, its review and the estimate. It should stop at **Start
   building**. The plan's page should say "Written by the dev plan author
   (*model*)".
5. Ask the chat agent "how is it going?" (it should call `get_timeline`) and
   "what did the plan reviewer say?" (`get_agent_run`).
6. Relay an approval of something, and check the page says "Approved by a
   person, relayed by the chat agent" with your words.

## Traps for the next session

- **Migration order.** `0011` is M10's and merges first. M9's `0012` is safe in
  either order, because the runner fills gaps.
- **New approve or send-back paths should call `approveDocumentAs` or
  `returnForChangesAs` with the verdict**, or the plain versions, which record
  an agent's verdict when there is a run and otherwise a person's escalation
  answer.
- **New registration surfaces should call `registerDocBy` or `reviseDocBy`**,
  saying who and from where. `RegisterDocForOwner` and `ReviseDoc` guess from
  the actor name.
- **A project whose `mcp_actor` equals its `ui_actor` now fails to load**, with
  a sentence naming the field. That includes `ui_actor: chat-agent` or
  `mcp_actor: operator`.
- **MCP results changed shape** for milestones, roadmaps and checklists: `id`
  is the ID, and `row_id` is the row id. Lookups accept either.
- **A project served from a long path won't start** (the 108-byte socket
  limit). The demo used `/var/tmp/m10demo`. `setup.sh` stops a previous demo
  server by the process id it saved.
- **`gofmt -l` still lists three files** (`internal/lifecycle/task_test.go`,
  `internal/server/integration_send_test.go`,
