    How to read this

    A review counts once an agent has reached a verdict: approve, ask for changes, or escalate to a person. Findings are the problems it raised; a verifier's unmet acceptance criteria count as major findings. Rounds count how many times the same document, task or feature came back to this reviewer. Time to verdict runs from the agent starting to its answer, so time spent waiting in the queue doesn't count.

    A reviewer is flagged once it has given at least {{.Rules.MinVerdicts}} verdicts, when:

    • it approves {{.Rules.HighPct}}% or more, its median verdict takes under {{.Rules.FastSeconds}} seconds, and its median answer is under {{.Rules.TerseOutput}} tokens long. Speed alone isn't a warning: a document review is a single call to the model, so a fast model is quick however carefully it reads;
    • it approves {{.Rules.HighPct}}% or more of its reviews and raises fewer than {{.Rules.FewPerReview}}, counting major and minor findings together. This isn't applied to verification, because a verifier can't approve while a criterion is unmet, so its approvals never carry findings;
    • it approves {{.Rules.LowPct}}% or fewer.

    People's decisions aren't counted here. They are on each document's page, and in the inbox.
